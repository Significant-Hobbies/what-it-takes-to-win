## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Four forms of luck
The product SHALL distinguish structural luck, encounter luck, event luck, and outcome variance in plain language.

#### Scenario: Visitor asks how luck matters
- **WHEN** they read the model, person comparison, or methodology
- **THEN** they can identify examples of each form and understand that luck can alter every stage transition

### Requirement: Honest unmeasured boundary
The product MUST state that the current successful-only biographical dataset cannot estimate how lucky a person was or how an outcome would change under a counterfactual event.

#### Scenario: Visitor interprets a similarity result
- **WHEN** visible profile overlap is displayed
- **THEN** the result identifies unobserved luck and variance as reasons the overlap is non-predictive
