## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Four-stage outcome model
The product SHALL explain early extraordinary outcomes as an observed path from starting conditions through converted leverage and trajectory to a selected milestone.

#### Scenario: First-time homepage visitor
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the homepage
- **THEN** the four stages are visible in plain language before the detailed charts

### Requirement: Defined editorial tiers
The product SHALL define tiers 1 through 4 as editorial descriptions of milestone significance and SHALL state that tiers do not measure human worth or future potential.

#### Scenario: Visitor encounters a tier label
- **WHEN** a tier is presented on an insight or person page
- **THEN** the visitor can access its name, meaning, and methodological caveat

### Requirement: Gradient and overlap
The product SHALL show the observed advantage and leverage summaries for every tier and SHALL show that their score ranges overlap.

#### Scenario: Visitor asks whether advantages determine tier
- **WHEN** the visitor opens the insight surface
- **THEN** the tier means, interquartile ranges, correlations, and overlap warning are presented together

### Requirement: Evidence-bounded language
The product SHALL describe dataset relationships as observations or associations and MUST NOT claim that the annotated scores cause or predict an outcome.

#### Scenario: Product summarizes a tier relationship
- **WHEN** an aggregate or personal result is rendered
- **THEN** it uses non-causal language and identifies the successful-only sample limitation
