# ROI lab surface brief

## Scope and mode

- Primary target: `src/pages/roi/index.astro`
- Related targets: `src/styles/roi.css`, `src/scripts/roi-calculator.ts`, `src/lib/roi.ts`
- Mode: Operate

## Audience and job

A person considering a consequential action needs to expose their own investment,
outcome values, and probabilities, then inspect the arithmetic without receiving
a prediction or recommendation.

## Task, proof, and constraints

- Primary task: complete a blank common-unit ledger and reconcile outcomes to 100%.
- Proof: visible formulas, row subtotals, outcome contributions, validation, and
  effort allocation all derive from the same local calculation.
- Constraint: never infer what success is worth, another person's effort, or an
  outcome probability; no entered value leaves the page.

## Direction and memorable moment

The existing evidence system becomes a reconciled worksheet: editable ledger at
left, calculation proof at right. The memorable moment is seeing the same total
effort split into foundation, capability-building, and final-mile work without
calling any stage lesser.
