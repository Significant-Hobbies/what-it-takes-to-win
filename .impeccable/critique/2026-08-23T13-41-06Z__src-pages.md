## Design Specificity Verdict

Field Atlas is highly product-specific. It translates the core argument—similar
coordinates do not imply equivalent routes—into one visual system that can
carry immersive explanation, path records, evidence ledgers, methodology,
comparison, and decision tools. It no longer resembles a generic dashboard or
an interchangeable editorial template.

The deterministic CLI scan found zero issues across `src/pages`. Browser
evidence initially found two real P1 accessibility defects: insufficient
contrast for small metadata and audit statuses whose meaning was only visual.
Both were fixed and reverified. Muted metadata now passes at 4.73:1 or better,
vermilion labels pass at 4.91:1 or better, and the accessibility tree announces
Complete, Partially complete, and Pending status text.

## Overall Impression

This now feels like a serious research publication with a memorable spatial
idea, not a decorated dataset. The strongest opportunity is no longer visual
identity; it is shortening or accelerating the few deliberately dense research
flows.

## What's Working

- The system changes form by job without losing authorship: Story is a route
  survey, Explore an index, profiles dossiers, Coverage a release ledger, and
  ROI a ruled worksheet.
- Evidence limitations stay beside the claim they qualify. The footer audit,
  Coverage verdict, questionnaire mechanism map, and ROI formulas are honest.
- Responsive behavior is stable at 390, 768, and 1440 pixels with no page-level
  horizontal overflow, correct active navigation, and orderly headings.

## Priority Issues

- **[P2] Dense flows lack accelerators.** Person dossiers and long comparison
  questionnaires ask for sustained scrolling. Add local section navigation or
  session resume only if usage evidence shows abandonment; do not turn the
  publication into a wizard by default. Suggested command: `$impeccable onboard`.
- **[P2] A few desktop controls are 42px high.** They pass WCAG 2.5.8 and all
  mobile controls reach 44px, but a future touch-polish pass could standardize
  the larger target. Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`.
- **[P3] Audit microcopy remains optically small.** The 9–10px labels now pass
  AA contrast and expose correct semantics, but they are intentionally dense.
  Raise them only if real-device reading shows strain. Suggested command:
  `$impeccable typeset`.
