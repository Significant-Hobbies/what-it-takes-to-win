## First invitation

Hi [name],

I'm Sarthak, the creator of Paths: https://paths.significanthobbies.com/.
It explores the work, support and unexpected turns behind a life—the things
we miss when we compare ourselves with someone's visible achievements.

I'd love to invite you to a conversation for a podcast pilot. Your experience
with [one specific, verified detail] made me curious about [one real question].
I'd like to hear what came before that moment, what helped, what was difficult,
and what the public story misses. You're welcome to challenge the project's
premise, too.

Would you be open to discussing it? We can agree the length, audio or video
format, and recording and publication terms before committing. You can skip
questions and review the quotes and claims drawn from the conversation before
publication.

Thanks,
Sarthak

## Question spine

1. What were you doing before the milestone people associate with you?
2. What work or practice made the next step possible?
3. Who helped, and what access or support was already there?
4. What constraints, setbacks or responsibilities does the public story miss?
5. Which encounter or change could you not have planned?
6. What might someone misunderstand if they tried to follow your path?
7. Where does the argument on Paths fail to describe your experience?

Keep recipient details and replies outside this public repository. Do not
invent audience numbers, published episodes or previous guests. Recording and
publication arrangements are still to be agreed; this invitation is not a
consent form.
