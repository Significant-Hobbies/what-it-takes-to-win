Intake ages and milestone dates originate in #81's screening register; they are not certified by this draft. Core eligibility remains first qualifying proof at 26–39 inclusive, with earlier qualifying proof in any domain disqualifying core inclusion. Ordinary education, employment and private practice do not establish either distinction or its absence.

## Five priority case notes

### 001 — Dominic O’Brien: memory sport

**Capability lane.** DO-01: O’Brien reports school difficulties and leaving at 16, then watching a card memorisation demonstration in 1987 at age 30 and teaching himself card memorisation [S109, opening autobiographical paragraphs]. This is testimony about schooling and a practice turning point, not proof of no prior excellence. DO-02: the competition organisation also places memory training at 30 in 1987 [S134, training paragraph]; independence from his account is unestablished.

**Proof lane.** DO-03: the secondary championship table lists O’Brien as the 1991 London winner [S135, “World Champions (1991–2016)”]. DO-04: S134 describes eight world titles and the competition's inception in 1991; it does not supply the original 1991 rules, field, date or result. The intake's age 34 remains unverified here. Do not infer a precise birth date from a rounded training age.

**Brought / handed / surrounded.** The demonstrated starting fact is his reported self-directed practice after seeing a television demonstration [DO-01]. Employment, financial runway, actual practice hours, coaching and who bore the cost are unknown. Later education work and titles are subsequent outcomes [S109/S134], not the first milestone.

**Hold and next public checks.** Retrieve primary 1991 results and age evidence; search pre-1991 selective results; audit employment, sport, arts, technical and community pursuits before 26. School reports in a retrospective account do not close those domains. No eligibility or evidence status is assigned.

### 004 — Nicola “Nicky” Spinks: fell running

**Capability lane.** NS-01: the federation retrospective describes farm running, horse riding and cycling, office work at 17, and renewed running at 19 [S110, “Early memories of running”]. NS-02: Wikipedia places her first competitive fell race in 2001 [S065, biography]. Starting a race is not itself qualifying proof; road running and earlier participation require checking.

**Proof lane.** NS-03: both retrieved biographies locate a Bob Graham Round completion in 2005 [S110, “Distance and fell running”; S065, running chronology]. S110 describes the 24-hour, 42-mountain, 66-mile challenge. N01 supplies a primary-record route, but no individual ratification record was retrieved. NS-04: S065 lists birth on 22 April 1967; a year-only 2005 result would imply 37–38, not a certified 38. Both the birth evidence and result date require primary corroboration. Later records in 2012–2015 [S065] must not replace an earlier qualifying result.

**Brought / handed / surrounded.** Farm familiarity and earlier recreational running are reported starting conditions [NS-01]. An advertisement in Runner’s World is a reported introduction to fell running [S110]. Quantified training hours, work schedule, support team and financial costs remain unknown. The interview's prose ordering is not a reliable dated sequence for every road/fell transition.

**Hold and next public checks.** Check club ratification and 2001–2004 race results, earlier road races and selection thresholds; resolve actual completion date and identity. Horse riding, school/work and other pre-26 domains remain open. Completion selectivity must be justified from the actual standard, not inferred from the difficulty description alone.

## Proposed reusable research record (specification only)

Store future evidence separately from immutable intake provenance. A draft record needs stable intake ID and aliases; birth evidence and date precision; claim IDs; exact source URLs/titles/publisher/author/publication/access dates; short paraphrases; testimony versus primary record flags; origin groups and dependence notes; explicit contradictory claims; and revision history. Each dated event must link claims and preserve acceptance, selection, filming, broadcast, release and later recognition as distinct events.

Keep capability/practice and external-proof lanes separate. Preserve the archive's brought/handed/surrounded framework as sourced descriptions, with unknowns for hours, runway, support and personal contribution. Add attempt/turning-point and subsequent-development fields without importing numeric legacy scores, inferring personality, or calculating eligibility probabilities. Store the comparison pool and selection standard in their actual domain context, without universal percentiles.

Each early-history domain requires a coverage state and the checks actually performed, claim links, gaps and contradictory evidence. A future human reviewer must determine whether a gap is an acceptable documented limitation. Public-source research alone must not set `approved`, `verified`, a second-review identity or a publication-clearing state. Draft authoring and automated checks are not independent editorial review.
