About this preview and its evidence

These are selected chronological excerpts, not complete biographies, a common age scale, or a causal model. Green marks documented early access/support; rust marks a later milestone. Neither encodes an amount of advantage.

Sources are linked next to each event. The archive's exact-age annotations need further checking: this preview corrects Serena's 1999 title age to 17 using the WTA record and uses the year 1975 rather than repeating the archive's Microsoft-founding age. The archive itself has not been edited.

The opening samples the product mechanism. The existing archive, articles, tools, and methodology remain unchanged. This is a static concept, not a production release.
