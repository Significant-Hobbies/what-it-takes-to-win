# Visual system

## Selected direction

**Kinetic Essay** was selected on 2026-08-23 for the complete public surface
system. The product is an independently published visual argument: documented
paths move through a continuous paper-and-ink composition, evidence is attached
to the course as annotation, and every comparison keeps its limits in view.

The previous dark dashboard and Field Atlas plus inset-race compositions are
anti-references. Do not preserve their rounded card grid, KPI-tile grammar,
permanent index rail, decorative metadata, colored container borders, or a dark
media rectangle pasted beside an unrelated page system. The race and argument
must occupy one continuous plane.

The approved direction probe is
`artifacts/design/marble-direction-probes/kinetic-essay-1440.png`.

## Product translation

- **Unique mechanism:** guide the reader through why comparison fails, then
  place documented paths beside one another while keeping what people brought,
  what they were handed, what surrounded them, perseverance, sequence, and luck
  separate.
- **Cultural home:** annotated long-form visual essays, research figures,
  editorial diagrams, and working-paper marginalia.
- **First-surface proof:** three marbles run the same course, trade the lead as
  the terrain changes, and expose why a visible finish never explains the
  conditions that produced it.
- **Audience scene:** a curious reader, writer, or researcher slows down after
  encountering a famous success story and needs an inspectable counterweight.
- **Register:** serious public research publication, not a dashboard, coaching
  tool, ranking system, fan archive, or prediction engine.

## Principles

1. **Course before containers.** Composition comes from one continuous route,
   its margin, annotations, and reading sequence—not stacks of generic cards.
2. **Evidence first.** Numbers, definitions, provenance, and limitations carry
   more visual weight than decoration.
3. **One question per passage.** Each major surface or section states the research
   question it answers and the evidence boundary that limits it.
4. **Different concepts look different.** Starting conditions, built leverage,
   sequence, and unscored luck use distinct line languages as well as color.
5. **Dense, not cramped.** The essay rewards scanning while keeping 65–75ch
   reading measure, generous section separation, and reachable touch targets.
6. **Human paths, not specimens.** Map language explains sequence and context;
   it never reduces a person to a coordinate, rank, or combined score.
7. **The boundary stays on the sheet.** Selected-outlier limits, missing control
   groups, confidence, and pending audits remain adjacent to the evidence they
   qualify.

## Material and color

- Main paper: warm mineral white `#f2f0e7`
- Alternate paper: cool white `#e8e7df`
- Editorial ink: `#1c1c19`
- Route blue / primary action: `#315f91`
- Leverage vermilion: `#a72e22`
- Sequence ochre: `#b58b32`
- Secondary text: `#65635d`; faint metadata: `#5f5c55`
- Hairline rule: `rgba(28, 28, 25, 0.28)`
- Dark interpretation note: editorial ink with paper-white type

Color names a route or evidence type. Containers do not receive semantic
border colors. Every concept also has a non-color distinction: solid blue for
starting conditions, broken vermilion for leverage, ochre sequence markers
for sequence, and a sparse black dash for luck or unmeasured territory.

The default surface is light because the use scene is reading a public essay in
normal ambient light. The race is drawn into that paper rather than isolated in
a dark media window.

## Typography

- Reading: a modern grotesk/system sans with strong lowercase and restrained
  geometry. Display: a sober book serif used only for the argument's major
  sentences and essay titles.
- Data and coordinates: system monospace only for IDs, dates, measurements,
  source states, and chart axes.
- Display tracking stops at `-0.04em`; body tracking remains normal.
- Display type is large and compact but never exceeds `6rem`.
- Kicker text is used only when it names a real plate, field edition, survey
  question, or evidence state. Repeated decorative eyebrows are prohibited.

## Composition

- Desktop story pages use a reading column beside a sticky evidence figure.
  Utility routes may use a wider ruled sheet without a permanent sidebar.
- Page headers are compact and quiet so the first argument begins immediately.
- The primary navigation stays short and follows the reading order: Journey,
  Evidence, Articles, Atlas.
  Secondary destinations move to the footer or contextual indexes.
- Major explanatory regions may share hairline rules like a fold-out legend.
  They do not become rounded cards.
- Person collections are ordered records or route entries. A record exposes
  the minimum useful metadata and a small route trace; it does not imitate a
  SaaS card.
- Profile pages use a route map, evidence key, field notes, source ledger, and
  explicit interpretation boundary.
- Methodology and coverage pages read as research protocols and field keys,
  with persistent local contents on wide screens.

## Components

- Primary actions are underlined route-blue text or compact solid controls.
  Secondary actions are quiet text links.
- Inputs sit directly on the sheet with square or lightly rounded geometry
  (`4px` maximum) and strong labels. A filter group is a field station, not a
  bordered panel.
- Interpretation boundaries are dark field notes set slightly off-axis only
  when the note remains readable and does not obstruct content.
- Tables use ruled ledger rows with sticky headings where needed; avoid an
  extra enclosing card when the table border already defines the surface.
- Charts include a visible title, accessible text label, route-consistent line
  grammar, and explicit definitions.
- Confidence and completeness remain separate. Pending evidence is marked by
  a dash pattern and written status, never a misleading completion badge.
- Focus rings use route blue and are never removed.

## Story surface

The homepage remains a five-chapter explanation of the visible finish, the
three sources of advantage, perseverance, luck, and the release from identity
comparison. A scroll-scrubbed SVG
marble race is the central demonstration; the Kinetic Essay system draws the
course, annotations, chapter figures, and interpretation boundaries onto one
continuous page:

- the permanent atlas rail is removed;
- three marbles share one open channel and visibly trade the lead as the course
  alternates between drops, flats, gates, and chance-derived deflectors;
- the race demonstrates path dependence rather than ranking: there is no winner
  table, podium, timer, score, or claim that one runner is intrinsically better;
- chapter figures stay visible above a dimmed race layer and explain survivor
  selection, starting conditions, leverage, sequence, and unscored luck;
- the first route starts inside the first opening at scroll zero;
- branch amplitude is hash-derived, never score-derived;
- the three condition factors are never summed anywhere in the product;
- position remains a pure function of scroll progress so reverse scrubbing is
  exact;
- the course is an inline SVG and remains complete without script;
- script only maps native scroll progress to SVG positions and active chapter
  annotations; it does not intercept scrolling or run an idle animation loop;
- reduced-motion readers receive a deliberate static race state with the same
  argument and all chapters remain in document flow;
- there is no WebGL runtime, remote asset, model, texture, or audio layer.

## Motion and responsive behavior

- One authored motion language: the marble race is scrubbed by the reader;
  route drawing, map registration, and field-note settling support it. Do not
  apply identical entrance animation to every section.
- Operational and evidence surfaces use hover/focus motion only.
- Every route respects reduced motion.
- At tablet widths, the index margin becomes a compact top folio and local
  contents. At phone widths, it becomes a short horizontal task index.
- Multi-column legends collapse to two columns, then one, while line samples
  and written definitions remain visible.
- Data tables and route diagrams may scroll horizontally; prose must not.
- Mobile prioritizes product name, current task, one primary action, readable
  records, and the interpretation boundary.

## Share surface
